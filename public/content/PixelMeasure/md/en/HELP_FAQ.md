# Pixel Measure - Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the Pixel Measure Help & FAQ page! Our goal is to help you measure your world seamlessly using the power of Augmented Reality, while keeping your data entirely private.

## Getting Started {: data-toc-key="getting_started" }

### 1. What permissions does Pixel Measure need?

Pixel Measure requires exactly one mandatory permission:
- **Camera:** We use your device's camera to enable Android's ARCore. This allows the app to "see" surfaces like floors and tables, letting you place virtual points to measure distances. **Your camera feed is never recorded or sent to the cloud.**

### 2. How do I make my first measurement?

1. Open the app and slowly move your phone from side to side. Point the camera at the floor or surface you want to measure.
2. Wait for the white tracking dots (the "mesh") to appear on the surface. This means the app has successfully mapped the area.
3. Aim the central reticle (the crosshair) at your starting point and tap the **"+"** button to place an anchor.
4. Move your phone to the endpoint and tap **"+"** again. A line will be drawn with the exact measurement!

## Tools & Object Placement {: data-toc-key="tools" }

### 1. How do I use Shapes (Squares & Triangles)?
Instead of placing multiple single points, you can use the **Shapes Tool** to measure entire areas instantly.
Select the Square or Triangle tool from the bottom menu, tap to place the center, and drag your phone to expand the shape's size. The app will automatically calculate the total area (e.g., square meters or square feet) and perimeter.

### 2. How does the Object Placement tool work?
Want to know if a new fridge will fit in your kitchen?
1. Open the **Objects Menu**.
2. Create a custom object (e.g., Width: 80cm, Height: 180cm, Depth: 70cm).
3. The app will generate a 3D bounding box matching those exact dimensions.
4. Drag and drop this box into your physical space through the camera view. If the virtual box fits, the real object will too!

## Accuracy & Best Practices {: data-toc-key="accuracy" }

### 1. Why are my measurements sometimes inaccurate?
AR relies on computer vision. For the best accuracy:
- **Ensure good lighting:** Dark rooms make it hard for the camera to track movement.
- **Avoid reflective surfaces:** Glass tables or highly polished floors confuse the depth sensors.
- **Move slowly:** Rapid phone movements can cause the AR engine to lose tracking.
- **Ensure texture:** Pure white, featureless walls are hard to track. The camera needs visual details (like wood grain, carpets, or tiles) to anchor objects properly.
