# Pixel Measure Roadmap {: data-toc-key="top" }

Welcome to the Pixel Measure roadmap! Our mission is to build the most accurate, intuitive, and privacy-respecting AR measurement tool for Android. This document outlines our recent launches and future directions.

We believe in continuous improvement. Your feedback is crucial to this process, so if you have ideas or suggestions, we'd love to hear them!

## ✅ Recently Launched {: data-toc-key="launched" }

This is the powerful foundation of Pixel Measure, available now.

### **The Core Experience**
- **Precision AR Tracking:** A highly optimized ARCore engine capable of detecting flat surfaces, walls, and complex lighting environments.
- **Dynamic Reticle & Snapping:** Intelligent crosshairs that snap to corners and edges for pixel-perfect line placement.
- **Shape Generator:** Automated tools to calculate the perimeter and total area of squares and triangles in real-time.
- **Absolute Privacy:** 100% on-device processing with zero camera recording or cloud uploading.

### **The Architectural Toolkit (Pixel Measure+)**
- **Unlimited Room History:** A robust local database allowing users to save and revisit multiple rooms and properties.
- **Custom 3D Object Library:** A tool to create virtual bounding boxes of exact dimensions to test furniture fitting before buying.
- **Professional Exports:** High-quality PDF and raw CSV export capabilities for sharing spatial data with contractors.
- **Premium Theming:** Support for AMOLED Black to conserve battery life during camera-heavy rendering.

## 🧭 Future Directions {: data-toc-key="future" }

Looking further ahead, these are some of the concepts we are exploring to make Pixel Measure even better.

### **Advanced Spatial Mapping**
- **2D Floorplan Generation:** The ability to auto-generate a top-down 2D floorplan drawing based on your AR points, complete with wall lengths and doors.
- **Multi-Room Connectivity:** A system to stitch multiple room sessions together into a single cohesive property map.
- **LiDAR Optimization:** Enhanced depth detection and instant meshing for newer Android devices equipped with hardware ToF (Time of Flight) sensors.

### **Usability Enhancements**
- **Voice Annotations:** Allow users to record a quick voice note (e.g., "Kitchen Window") to attach to specific measurement lines.
- **Cloud Backup (Opt-In):** An optional, end-to-end encrypted Google Drive backup system for users who want to safely migrate their room layouts to a new device.
