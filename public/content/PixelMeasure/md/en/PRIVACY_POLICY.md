# Privacy Policy for Pixel Measure

**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

At **fertwbr**, we believe that what you measure in your personal space belongs only to you. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict privacy-first approach regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the Pixel Measure mobile application (the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Core Privacy Principle: On-Device Processing

Pixel Measure is an Augmented Reality (AR) application that requires access to your device's camera to function. **We have designed this app so that your camera feed and measurement data are never sent to our servers.**

* **No Video Recording:** The app processes camera frames locally in real-time to detect surfaces and objects using Android's ARCore. We do not record, upload, or store your camera feed or photos on any remote server.
* **Local Data Storage:** All your saved dimensions, created shapes (squares, triangles), object placements, and room histories are stored strictly locally in your device's internal storage or your personal cloud backup (if enabled by you on your Android device).

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

To ensure maximum privacy, Pixel Measure **DOES NOT**:
* Collect or transmit your camera feed, pictures, or video.
* Create or upload a remote 3D map or point cloud of your home.
* Track your location via GPS or Wi-Fi.
* Use targeted advertising frameworks or third-party behavioral trackers.
* Require you to create an account or provide personal identifiers (like an email or phone number).

## 3. Permissions We Request

To provide the Service, Pixel Measure requires the following device permissions:

* **Camera:** Strictly required to enable Augmented Reality (ARCore). Used exclusively for real-time surface detection and placing virtual measuring points.
* **Storage / Media (Optional):** Requested only if you choose to export your measurements (e.g., as PDF, CSV, or images) to your device's downloads folder.

## 4. Third-Party Services and APIs

While our app processes AR data locally, we rely on standard infrastructure provided by Google to operate the app:

* **Google ARCore:** Pixel Measure uses Google Play Services for AR to understand the physical environment. ARCore processes visual data locally on your device. For more details on how Google handles ARCore data, please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Google Play Billing:** If you choose to upgrade to Pixel Measure+, payment processing and subscription management are securely handled by Google Play. We do not have access to your credit card information.
* **Crashlytics / Vitals (Opt-in):** We may collect anonymous, aggregated crash reports (e.g., device model, OS version, and the line of code that failed) solely to fix bugs. This data contains no personal information or AR data.

## 5. User Control and Data Deletion

Since all measurement histories, custom objects, and room layouts are saved locally on your device:
* **You have absolute control.** You can delete specific measurements or clear your entire history directly within the app settings.
* **Uninstalling the App:** If you uninstall Pixel Measure, all associated local measurement data will be permanently deleted from your device (unless you use Android's built-in app backup).

## 6. Children's Privacy and Digital Safety

Our Service complies with international digital safety frameworks, including the Brazilian **ECA Digital (Lei nº 15.211/2025)** and the **LGPD**.
Pixel Measure is a technical utility tool. We do not profile users, we do not employ engagement manipulation (dark patterns), and we do not collect any personal data from anyone, including individuals under the age of 18.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
